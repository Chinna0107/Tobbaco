# Tobacco-Themed UI Color Scheme

## Color Palette (Based on Natural Tobacco Aesthetic)

### Primary Colors
- **Tobacco Brown**: #8B4513 (Saddle Brown)
- **Dark Brown**: #654321
- **Light Brown**: #A0522D (Sienna)

### Secondary Colors
- **Tobacco Green**: #2E7D32 (Forest Green)
- **Light Green**: #4CAF50
- **Dark Green**: #1B5E20

### Accent Colors
- **Gold**: #DAA520 (Goldenrod) - Replace all purple/blue accents
- **Cream**: #F5DEB3 (Wheat) - Replace white text
- **Tan**: #D2B48C - Secondary text

### Background Colors
- **Dark**: #1a1410 (Very dark brown) - Replace dark navy
- **Medium**: #2d2416 - Replace medium navy
- **Light**: #3d3020 - Cards and sections
